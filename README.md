# Prospector II

A sci-fi roguelike about exploring space, surveying planets, managing a crew, and earning enough to retire.

Survival is uncertain. Accidents, pirates, monsters, hostile worlds and bad luck can end an expedition early. Better equipment and experienced crew improve your chances.

## Play

- [Play in your browser](https://sebo2203.github.io/Prospector-II/) — the newest published experimental game, now on `main`. [Browser version](package.json).
- [Download the standalone HTML](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sebo2203/Prospector-II/main/index.html) and open the saved file in your browser for offline play.
- [Download the Windows desktop app](https://sebo2203.github.io/Prospector-II/downloads/Prospector-II-v0.26-portable.exe) — the older v0.26 build; it does not contain the latest browser changes.

Explore, survey, trade, salvage or fight to fund your expedition. Choose your risks, look after your crew, and decide when to return home.
